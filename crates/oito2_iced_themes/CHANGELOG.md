### Added

- `Preset` with the 13 color presets of oito2_ui: Everforest Light and Dark, Rosé Pine, Rosé Pine Moon and Rosé Pine Dawn, Nord Dark and Light, Dracula and Alucard, and Catppuccin Latte, Frappé, Macchiato and Mocha.
- `Preset::theme` builds the theme of a preset at standard or high contrast; labels, stable names and lookup by name, brightness and filtering by brightness.
- `palettes` with the official palettes of each family; Catppuccin comes from the `catppuccin` crate.
