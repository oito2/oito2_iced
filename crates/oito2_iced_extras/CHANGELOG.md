## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `qr_code`: QR codes with the same matrices as oito2_ui, square or rounded modules, a center image and error correction levels.
- `signature_pad`: a signature pad with stroke width by speed, undo and clear, and export to SVG and PNG.
- `image_cropper`: zoom, pan, quarter turns, mirroring, aspect ratios and a circular mask, with a live preview and a `crop` function that returns the cropped image.
- `navigation_menu`: a navigation menu with link and panel items, hover delays, a sliding selection line and keyboard support.
